# Specialized Effects

Effects for special jobs: vocoding, adding brightness, a sub octave, widening.

In this chapter: **Vocoder**, **Exciter**, **Sub Bass**.

## Vocoder

![Vocoder](img/fx-vocoder.jpg)

The classic vocoder: the track's spectrum (bands followed) shapes a carrier - the side chain or a built-in synth.

> **How to use it:** Put it on a synth track, set the side chain to your vocal track, and play chords: the synth talks.

### Controls

**Bands** (8, 12, 16, 24, 32) · **Lowest** (50 to 500 Hz) · **Highest** (2000 to 14000 Hz) · **Attack** (0.5 to 50 ms) · **Release** (5 to 500 ms) · **Formant** (-12 to 12 st) · **Carrier** (Side Chain, Saw, Saw + Noise, Noise) · **Carrier Note** (C0, C#0, D0, D#0, E0, F0, F#0, G0, G#0, A0, ...) · **Unvoiced** (0 to 100 %) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %)

## Exciter

![Exciter](img/fx-exciter.jpg)

Adds brightness: new harmonics made from the highs only.

> **How to use it:** A little on a dull vocal or overheads adds air and presence.

### Controls

**Frequency** (1000 to 12000 Hz) · **Harmonics** (0 to 100 %) · **Amount** (0 to 100 %) · **Color** (Color 1, Color 2) · **Dry Signal** (Off, On) · **High Ratio** (0 to 100 %)

## Sub Bass

![Sub Bass](img/fx-subBass.jpg)

An octave (or two) below the bass, made from its zero crossings and following its level.

> **How to use it:** Adds weight under a thin bass line or kick; keep it subtle and check on headphones.

### Controls

**Bass Range** (60 to 300 Hz) · **Octave** (-1, -2) · **Sub Level** (-60 to 6 dB) · **Dry Level** (-60 to 6 dB) · **Shape** (Sine, Square)
