# Amps and Pedals

Guitar and bass amps, cabinets, microphones and stomp boxes. **How to use them:** record your guitar or bass clean through a DI (the instrument input of your interface), then choose the amp and pedals afterwards - you can change the sound at any time.

In this chapter: **Amp Designer**, **Bass Amp Designer**, **Pedalboard**.

## Amp Designer

![Amp Designer](img/fx-guitarAmp.jpg)

Amp Designer: 24 amps, 25 cabinets (and Direct), an EQ type for the tone stack, gain, bass, mids, treble, presence, master; spring and studio reverbs (ten types, level); tremolo or vibrato (depth, speed or synced); seven microphones, where they point and how close; output.

> **How to use it:** Choose the amp, then the cabinet and microphone - moving the mic changes the tone as much as the amp's knobs.

## Pedalboard

![Pedalboard](img/fx-pedalboard.jpg)

Pedalboard: a chain of up to six stomp boxes, in order, each switched on or off with its three knobs. 23 pedals: fuzz, octave fuzz, overdrive, tube drive, distortion, grinder, bit crusher, compressor, chorus, flanger, phaser, microphaser, tremolo, vibrato, scanner vibrato, rotary, auto wah, fuzz wah, tape delay, echo, spring reverb, ring shifter and pitch.

> **How to use it:** Stack a compressor, overdrive, chorus and delay like a guitarist's pedal board.
