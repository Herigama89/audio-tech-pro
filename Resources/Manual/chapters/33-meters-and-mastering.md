# Meters and Mastering

Meters and analyzers that show what you hear: levels, loudness, stereo image, tuning, tempo - and the Mastering Assistant.

In this chapter: **Level Meter**, **Loudness Meter**, **Correlation Meter**, **MultiMeter**, **Tuner**, **BPM Counter**, **Mastering Assistant**.

## Level Meter

![Level Meter](img/fx-levelMeter.jpg)

Level Meter: peak and RMS of each side, with the highest peak held (for the window).

### Controls

**Reset Hold** (Off, On)

## Loudness Meter

![Loudness Meter](img/fx-loudnessMeter.jpg)

Loudness Meter: M, S and I in LUFS, the loudness range in LU, the true peak; Start/Pause and Reset.

> **How to use it:** Play the whole song and read I (integrated): -14 LUFS for streaming.

### Controls

**Start/Pause** (Paused, Running) · **Reset** (Off, On) · **Scale** (EBU +9, EBU +18)

## Correlation Meter

![Correlation Meter](img/fx-correlationMeter.jpg)

Correlation Meter: +1 in phase (mono), 0 wide, -1 out of phase.

## MultiMeter

![MultiMeter](img/fx-multiMeter.jpg)

MultiMeter: a 31-band third-octave analyzer, a goniometer, correlation and peak levels.

> **How to use it:** Check the tonal balance and stereo image of your mix against a reference song.

### Controls

**Peak Hold** (Off, On) · **View** (Analyzer, Goniometer) · **Tilt** (Off, 1.5 dB/oct, 3 dB/oct, 4.5 dB/oct, 6 dB/oct)

## Tuner

![Tuner](img/fx-tuner.jpg)

Tuner: the note played and how far off it is, against the reference pitch.

> **How to use it:** Put it on a guitar or bass track and tune before recording.

### Controls

**Reference** (415 to 466 Hz)

## BPM Counter

![BPM Counter](img/fx-bpmCounter.jpg)

BPM Counter: the tempo found from the onsets of what's playing.

## Mastering Assistant

![Mastering Assistant](img/fx-masteringAssistant.jpg)

Mastering Assistant: listens to the mix (the first 30 seconds played after Reanalyze), then sets a corrective EQ (Auto EQ, how much of it), the gain to reach the loudness you choose, and runs the mix through a character - Clean, Valve, Punch or Transparent - into a true peak limiter at -1 dBTP; Excite adds upper-mid saturation, Width moves the sides (above 150 Hz), a custom EQ (low, mid, high); Loudness Compensation plays it back at the original loudness for a fair comparison.

> **How to use it:** Put it last on the master. Play the loudest part after Reanalyze; then choose a character and adjust Loudness by ear.

### Controls

**Character** (Clean, Valve, Punch, Transparent) · **Auto EQ** (0 to 100 %) · **Loudness** (-24 to -6 LUFS) · **Loudness Compensation** (Off, On) · **Excite** (Off, On) · **Width** (0 to 200 %) · **Custom EQ** (Off, On) · **Custom Low** (-6 to 6 dB) · **Custom Mid** (-6 to 6 dB) · **Custom High** (-6 to 6 dB) · **Reanalyze** (Off, On) · **Bypass** (Off, On)
