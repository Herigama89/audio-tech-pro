# Recorded Instruments

The recorded instruments of the **Nemix Library**, played by Nemix's SAMPLE layer. Each note was recorded by real players on real instruments, at several strengths, and many with alternating takes (round robins).

## Piano

- **Grand Piano** - Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC-BY 3.0)
- **Upright Piano** - Upright Piano KW, FreePats (CC0)

## Keys

- **Electric Piano (Rhodes)** - Rhodes Mark I by Jeff Learman (CC0)
- **Electric Grand** - Electric grand by Greg Sullivan (CC0)
