# Live Loops

**View > Live Loops** shows a grid of loops: one row per track, one column per **scene**. Use it to try arrangements, jam, or perform live.

![Live Loops](img/live-loops.jpg)

- **Click a cell** to start its loop on the next bar. It loops until you stop it, and meanwhile it replaces that track's timeline. Click it again to stop it at the next bar.
- **The numbers on top** start a whole scene - every loop in that column.
- **An empty cell** takes the selected region when you click it. Right-click a cell to **Put the selected region here**, **Clear cell**, or **Copy cell to the tracks area at the playhead**.
- **Cycle to Scene...** - copies what's on the tracks inside the cycle into a scene's cells: a quick way to fill a scene with your verse or chorus.
- **Stop All** - stops every loop.

## A way to work

1. Make a 4- or 8-bar idea on several tracks and set the cycle over it.
2. **Cycle to Scene** into scene 1.
3. Change the parts (a new drum pattern, a different bass) and capture them into scene 2, 3...
4. Play scenes in different orders to find the arrangement, then copy the cells you like into the tracks area.
