# The Channel Strip

Every channel has a full console channel strip built in - filters, a four-band EQ, a compressor and a gate/expander - before its insert effects. Click the **EQ** key at the top of a mixer strip to open it.

![The built-in channel strip](img/channel-strip.jpg)

## Its parts, top to bottom

- **Analyzer** - the sound's spectrum with the EQ curve over it. Choose the view (bars or line), the resolution, the range (dB), the speed and how long peaks hold.
- **Input / Output** - the level going in and coming out. **EQ in** and **DYN in** switch the EQ and the dynamics in or out (to compare).
- **Filters** - **HPF** (high pass: removes rumble and low mud below the frequency; 80 Hz is good for vocals and guitars) and **LPF** (low pass: removes harsh highs).
- **EQ** - four bands:
  - **HF** (high frequencies, a shelf or a **Bell**) - air and brightness.
  - **HMF** (high mids, with frequency and **Q**, the width) - presence, harshness.
  - **LMF** (low mids) - body, boxiness, mud.
  - **LF** (low frequencies, a shelf or a **Bell**) - weight and boom.
- **Compressor** - **Comp** is the amount (turn up to compress more), **Ratio** (Auto, or a fixed ratio), **Release**, and **Fast** for a quicker attack. It evens out the level so the track sits steady in the mix.
- **Gate / Expander** - **Threshold** (below it, the sound is turned down), **Range** (by how much), **Gate Rel** (how fast it closes), and **Exp** for a gentler expander instead of a gate. Use it to cut noise and spill between notes or hits.
- **Meters** - **IN**, **GR** (gain reduction: how much the compressor and gate are turning down) and **OUT**.

## How to use it

1. Start with the **HPF**: raise it until the sound gets thin, then back off a little.
2. **Cut before you boost**: find an ugly frequency by boosting a narrow band and sweeping, then cut it.
3. Add **Comp** until the GR meter moves 3 to 6 dB on the loud parts.
4. Use **EQ in / DYN in** to compare with and without - it should sound better, not just louder.

## The console

**Console** at the top right of the mixer chooses the channel strip on every channel: **Built-in**, or a channel-strip plugin you own (any of your plugins). The same strip then appears on every track, as on a real console. **Mix > Reset Channels...** puts every strip back to a fresh start.

## Smart Controls

**Smart Controls** (`B`) show eight knobs for the selected track's sound under the tracks: its volume, pan, instrument (cutoff, envelope...) and channel strip EQ. **Right-click a knob** to choose another parameter for it - any control of the track's plugins. **Default Mapping** puts them back.
