# Modulation Effects

Modulation effects move the sound in time: chorus and ensemble thicken, flanger and phaser sweep, tremolo and auto pan pulse, the rotor cabinet spins like an organ speaker. **How to use them:** start with the rate slow and the mix low, then raise until it's an effect you hear on purpose.

In this chapter: **Chorus**, **Ensemble**, **Flanger**, **Microphaser**, **Phaser**, **Tremolo**, **Auto Pan**, **Rotor Cabinet**, **Ringshifter**, **Scanner Vibrato**, **Vibrato**.

## Chorus

![Chorus](img/fx-chorus.jpg)

Chorus: rate, intensity, delay, voices, spread, feedback, D-Mode, mix.

> **How to use it:** On clean guitars, electric pianos and pads for width and shimmer.

### Controls

**Rate** (0.02 to 8 Hz) · **Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Delay** (3 to 40 ms) · **Voices** (1, 2, 3, 4) · **Spread** (0 to 100 %) · **Feedback** (0 to 80 %) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **D-Mode** (Off, On)

## Ensemble

![Ensemble](img/fx-ensemble.jpg)

Ensemble: up to eight voices modulated by LFO 1, LFO 2 and a random source (each on/off, rate, intensity), spread across the stereo field, their phases offset, volume compensation, mix.

### Controls

**Voices** (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8) · **Stereo Spread** (0 to 200 %) · **Output Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **LFO 1** (Off, On) · **LFO 1 Rate** (0.05 to 10 Hz) · **LFO 1 Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **LFO 2** (Off, On) · **LFO 2 Rate** (0.05 to 20 Hz) · **LFO 2 Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Random** (Off, On) · **Random Rate** (0.05 to 10 Hz) · **Random Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Phase** (0 to 360 °) · **Volume Compensation** (0 to 100 %)

## Flanger

![Flanger](img/fx-flanger.jpg)

Flanger: a very short delay swept by an LFO (rate or a note value), with intensity, feedback for the jet sound, stereo phase and mix.

### Controls

**Sync** (Off, On) · **Rate** (0.02 to 10 Hz) · **Note** (1/64, 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, ...) · **Delay** (0.1 to 10 ms) · **Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Feedback** (-100 to 100 %) · **Stereo Phase** (0 to 180 °) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %)

## Microphaser

![Microphaser](img/fx-microphaser.jpg)

Microphaser: a small, simple phaser - rate (or synced), intensity, feedback.

### Controls

**Sync** (Off, On) · **Rate** (0.02 to 10 Hz) · **Note** (1/64, 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, ...) · **Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Feedback** (0 to 100 %)

## Phaser

![Phaser](img/fx-phaser.jpg)

Phaser: 4 to 12 stages (odd numbers: comb filtering), the sweep between Floor and Ceiling by two LFOs (each its rate or a note, mixed), or by the input's envelope; the stereo phase and its distribution; feedback level, Warmth, a low/high cut filter, output mix.

### Controls

**Stages** (4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12) · **LFO 1 Sync** (Off, On) · **LFO 1 Rate** (0.02 to 10 Hz) · **LFO 1 Note** (1/64, 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, ...) · **Ceiling** (200 to 18000 Hz) · **Floor** (20 to 5000 Hz) · **Level** (-100 to 100 %) · **Phase** (0 to 360 °) · **Output Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Sweep Mode** (LFO, Envelope) · **LFO 2 Rate** (0.02 to 10 Hz) · **LFO 2 Sync** (Off, On) · **LFO 2 Note** (1/64, 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, ...) · **LFO Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Distribution** (Circular, Left/Right, Random) · **Warmth** (Off, On) · **Filter** (Off, On) · **Low Cut** (20 to 2000 Hz) · **High Cut** (1000 to 20000 Hz)

## Tremolo

![Tremolo](img/fx-tremolo.jpg)

Tremolo: rate (or a note), depth, the wave's smoothing and symmetry, its offset, and the left-right phase with its distribution.

### Controls

## Auto Pan

![Auto Pan](img/fx-autoPan.jpg)

Auto Pan: equal-power balance swung left and right in a chosen shape.

### Controls

**Sync** (Off, On) · **Rate** (0.05 to 20 Hz) · **Note** (1/64, 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, ...) · **Depth** (0 to 100 %) · **Shape** (Sine, Triangle, Square, Saw, Random)

## Rotor Cabinet

![Rotor Cabinet](img/fx-rotary.jpg)

Rotor Cabinet: Slow, Brake or Fast; the cabinet (Wood, Proline, Single, Split, and their "& Horn IR" versions); acceleration and maximum rate; motor control (Normal, Inverse, 910, Sync); the horn's and drum's microphones (Dynamic, Condenser or Mid-Side), front or rear, distance, angle, horn/drum balance, and the horn's and drum's width.

> **How to use it:** On organs: switch between Slow and Fast during the song (automate it) for the classic Leslie sound.

### Controls

**Rotation** (Slow, Brake, Fast) · **Max Rate** (4 to 9 Hz) · **Acceleration** (0.2 to 6 s) · **Balance** (0 to 100 %) · **Doppler** (0 to 100 %) · **Drive** (0 to 100 %) · **Horn Width** (0 to 100 %) · **Cabinet** (Wood, Proline, Single, Split, Wood & Horn IR, Proline & Horn IR, Split & Horn IR) · **Motor Control** (Normal, Inv, 910, Sync) · **Upper Mic** (Dynamic, Condenser, Mid-Side) · **Lower Mic** (Dynamic, Condenser, Mid-Side) · **Mic Position** (Front, Rear) · **Distance** (0 to 100 %) · **Angle** (0 to 180 °) · **Drum Width** (0 to 100 %)

## Ringshifter

![Ringshifter](img/fx-ringMod.jpg)

Ringshifter: a frequency shifter (one shift, or opposite shifts left and right) or a ring modulator (by its sine oscillator, or by the side chain). The oscillator's frequency, moved by the envelope follower and the LFO; a delay (or synced) with its level; the envelope follower (sensitivity, attack, decay) and the LFO (symmetry, smoothing, rate or synced); then dry/wet (also moved by the envelope and LFO), feedback and stereo width.

### Controls

**Mode** (Single Freq Shift, Dual Freq Shift, OSC Ring Mod, Side Chain Ring Mod) · **Frequency** (-5000 to 5000 Hz) · **Freq Env Follow** (-100 to 100 %) · **Freq LFO** (-100 to 100 %) · **Delay Time** (0 to 2000 ms) · **Delay Sync** (Off, On) · **Delay Note** (1/64, 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, ...) · **Delay Level** (0 to 100 %) · **Env Follower** (Off, On) · **Sensitivity** (0 to 100 %) · **Attack** (0.1 to 500 ms) · **Decay** (1 to 2000 ms) · **LFO** (Off, On) · **Symmetry** (-100 to 100 %) · **Smooth** (0 to 100 %) · **LFO Rate** (0.02 to 20 Hz) · **LFO Sync** (Off, On) · **LFO Note** (1/64, 1/32, 1/16T, 1/16, 1/16., 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4T, 1/4, ...) · **Dry/Wet** (0 to 100 %) · **Feedback** (0 to 100 %) · **Stereo Width** (0 to 200 %) · **Dry/Wet Env Follow** (-100 to 100 %) · **Dry/Wet LFO** (-100 to 100 %)

### Controls
