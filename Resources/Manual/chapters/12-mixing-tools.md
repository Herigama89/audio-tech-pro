# Mixing Tools

## Automix

**Mix > Automix...** (or **Automix** on the mixer) listens to every track and sets a balanced starting mix for a genre - in one undo step.

![Automix](img/automix.jpg)

- **Genre** - the style to balance for: how loud the kick, bass, vocal and keys sit against each other.
- **Listen to** - the whole song, or just the cycle (a chorus, say: quicker).
- **Faders** - each track's fader set to where its part sits in the genre.
- **Panning** - kick, snare, bass and lead vocal in the middle, doubled guitars left and right, backing vocals spread.
- **Plugins** - adds the plugins each part usually gets in the genre: EQ, compression set from its own level, de-essing, saturation on an 808, tuning on a lead vocal - ready to adjust.

Then fine-tune by ear. `Cmd/Ctrl+Z` puts it all back.

## Bypass All (Compare)

The **Bypass** key in the control bar (**Mix > Bypass All**) lets you hear the mix with every channel's processing switched off - preamps, channel strips, inserts, tape - and the master's inserts, bus compressor and tape. Click again for it all back. Use it to check that your processing really makes it better.

## The master bus compressor

**Bus Comp** on the mixer opens the master bus compressor: a console-style stereo compressor that "glues" the mix together.

![The master bus compressor](img/bus-comp.jpg)

- **Threshold** - lower it until the needle moves 1 to 3 dB on the loud parts.
- **Make-up** - turns the result back up.
- **Attack** and **Release** - slower attack lets the drums punch through; **Auto** release follows the music.
- **Ratio** - 2:1 or 4:1 for glue.
- **Mix** - blends in the uncompressed mix (parallel compression).
- **Grit** and **COLOR** - the console's saturation, from warm weight to a pushed edge.
- **IN** - switches it in or out. You can also choose any compressor plugin you own instead.

## MultiMeter

**Mix > MultiMeter**, or **MM** on the master strip: the master's levels, loudness and stereo image.

![MultiMeter](img/multimeter.jpg)

- **Analyzer** - 31 or 63 bands. **Tilt** lifts the bars above 1 kHz (4.5 dB/oct makes a balanced mix look flat). **Return Rate** and **Peak** set how fast bars fall and peak marks stay; **Hold** keeps them until **Reset**.
- **LUFS** - Momentary, Short-term and Integrated loudness; **L** and **R** levels as Peak, RMS or both.
- **Goniometer** - the stereo picture: a vertical line is mono, a wide cloud is wide.
- **Correlation** - +1 mono, 0 wide, below 0 out of phase (it will disappear on a mono speaker).

![The goniometer](img/goniometer.jpg)

## Reset Channels

**Mix > Reset Channels...** puts every channel (or the selected ones) and the master back to a fresh start: faders, pans, mute and solo, the channel strips, inserts and sends. Useful to start a mix again from scratch.

## Signal flow, groups and more

- **Mix > Signal Flow** - the selected track's whole path, stage by stage.
- **Mix > Group Settings...** - what each group links.
- **Mix > Exclusive Solo** - one solo at a time.
- **View > Show Comments in Mixer** - a notes box under each channel.

## Mixing tips

- Balance with **faders and pan first**, effects later.
- Check the mix **quietly** and in **mono** (the **Gain** plugin's Mono switch on the master).
- Leave **headroom**: the master should peak around -6 dB before mastering.
- Compare often with **Bypass All** and with a song you like.
