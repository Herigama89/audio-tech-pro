# Legacy Effects

Older designs kept for their sound and for older projects: simpler reverbs, compressors, gates and amps.

In this chapter: **EQ**, **Denoiser**, **AVerb**, **GoldVerb**, **Silver Compressor**, **Silver Gate**, **DeEsser**, **Speech Enhancer**, **Grooveshifter**, **Guitar Amp Pro**, **Bass Amp**.

## EQ

![EQ](img/fx-eq.jpg)

The simple three-band EQ: a low shelf, a mid bell (with Q) and a high shelf, each with its frequency and gain (up to 18 dB).

### Controls

**Low Freq** (20 to 1000 Hz) · **Low** (-18 to 18 dB) · **Mid Freq** (100 to 10000 Hz) · **Mid** (-18 to 18 dB) · **Mid Q** (0.2 to 10) · **High Freq** (1000 to 18000 Hz) · **High** (-18 to 18 dB)

## Denoiser

![Denoiser](img/fx-denoiser.jpg)

Spectral noise reduction: each frequency below the threshold is turned down, smoothly over time.

### Controls

**Threshold** (-100 to -20 dB) · **Reduction** (0 to 40 dB) · **Smoothing** (0 to 100 %)

## AVerb

![AVerb](img/fx-aVerb.jpg)

AVerb: one Density/Time control for both the reflections and the tail.

### Controls

**Predelay** (0 to 200 ms) · **Reflectivity** (0 to 100 %) · **Room Size** (0 to 100 %) · **Density/Time** (0 to 100 %) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %)

## GoldVerb

![GoldVerb](img/fx-goldVerb.jpg)

GoldVerb: early reflections from a room of 3 to 7 corners, and a separate tail, balanced.

### Controls

**Predelay** (0 to 200 ms) · **Room Shape** (3 Corners, 4 Corners, 5 Corners, 6 Corners, 7 Corners) · **Room Size** (1 to 50 m) · **Stereo Base** (0 to 200 cm) · **Initial Delay** (0 to 200 ms) · **Spread** (0 to 200 %) · **High Cut** (200 to 20000 Hz) · **Density** (0 to 100 %) · **Reverb Time** (0.3 to 20 s) · **Diffusion** (0 to 100 %) · **Balance ER/Reverb** (-100 to 100 %) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %)

## Silver Compressor

![Silver Compressor](img/fx-silverComp.jpg)

Silver Compressor: threshold, ratio, attack, release, a soft knee and makeup gain.

### Controls

**Threshold** (-60 to 0 dB) · **Ratio** (1 to 30 :1) · **Attack** (0.1 to 200 ms) · **Release** (5 to 2000 ms) · **Gain** (0 to 30 dB) · **Auto Gain** (Off, On) · **Soft Knee** (Off, On)

## Silver Gate

![Silver Gate](img/fx-silverGate.jpg)

Silver Gate: below the threshold the sound drops by the reduction, with attack, hold and release, and lookahead.

### Controls

**Threshold** (-100 to 0 dB) · **Reduction** (-100 to 0 dB) · **Attack** (0 to 100 ms) · **Hold** (0 to 2000 ms) · **Release** (2 to 2000 ms) · **Lookahead** (0 to 10 ms)

## DeEsser

![DeEsser](img/fx-deesserLegacy.jpg)

The original DeEsser: a detector band and a separate suppressor band; strength, smoothing, monitors.

### Controls

**Detector Frequency** (500 to 20000 Hz) · **Sensitivity** (0 to 100 %) · **Suppressor Frequency** (500 to 20000 Hz) · **Strength** (0 to 50 dB) · **Smoothing** (1 to 200 ms) · **Monitor** (Off, Detector, Suppressor)

## Speech Enhancer

![Speech Enhancer](img/fx-speechEnhancer.jpg)

Speech Enhancer: noise taken down, the microphone's colour corrected, the voice brought forward.

### Controls

**Denoise** (0 to 100 dB) · **Mic Correction** (Off, On) · **Mic Model** (Generic, Laptop, Desktop, Webcam, Headset) · **Voice Enhance** (Off, On) · **Enhance Mode** (Female Solo, Male Solo, Female Voice Over, Male Voice Over)

## Grooveshifter

![Grooveshifter](img/fx-grooveshifter.jpg)

Grooveshifter: swing put into straight playing - every other eighth (or sixteenth) moved later, without changing the pitch (grains of the sound played at their speed while time is stretched and squeezed).

### Controls

**Source Material** (Beat, Tonal) · **Grain Size** (0 to 100 ms) · **Grid** (1/8, 1/16) · **Swing** (50 to 85 %) · **Accent** (-12 to 12 dB)

## Guitar Amp Pro

![Guitar Amp Pro](img/fx-guitarAmpPro.jpg)

Guitar Amp Pro: its amps, speakers and EQs are among Amp Designer's.

### Controls
