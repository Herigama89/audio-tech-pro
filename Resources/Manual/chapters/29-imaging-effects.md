# Imaging Effects

Imaging effects place sound in the stereo field: wider, narrower, moved left or right. **How to use them:** widen pads, backing vocals and effects; keep bass, kick and lead vocal in the centre. Check the mix in mono.

In this chapter: **Stereo Spread**, **Direction Mixer**, **Spreader**.

## Stereo Spread

![Stereo Spread](img/fx-stereoSpread.jpg)

Widens by sending alternate frequency bands left and right (mono-compatible: it only adds side).

> **How to use it:** On pads and backing vocals; keep it off bass and kick.

### Controls

**Bands** (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8) · **Lower Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Upper Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Lower Freq** (80 to 2000 Hz) · **Upper Freq** (1000 to 18000 Hz) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %)

## Direction Mixer

![Direction Mixer](img/fx-directionMixer.jpg)

Direction Mixer: an LR or MS input, the direction its centre points to (degrees: 90 hard left, -90 hard right, beyond that turning back) and its spread (0 mono, 1 as is, 2 only the sides); Split gives the lows and highs (either side of the crossover) their own direction and spread.

### Controls

**Input** (Left/Right, Mid/Side) · **Spread (High)** (0 to 2) · **Direction (High)** (-180 to 180 °) · **Output** (-24 to 12 dB) · **Split** (Off, On) · **Crossover** (50 to 5000 Hz) · **Direction Low** (-180 to 180 °) · **Spread Low** (0 to 2)

## Spreader

![Spreader](img/fx-spreader.jpg)

Wider: each side a few milliseconds apart and a few cents apart, gently moving.

### Controls

**Delay** (0 to 30 ms) · **Pitch Spread** (0 to 30 cents) · **Rate** (0.05 to 3 Hz) · **Width** (0 to 200 %) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %)
