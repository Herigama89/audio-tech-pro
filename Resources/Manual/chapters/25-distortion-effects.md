# Distortion Effects

Distortion adds harmonics: warmth and weight at low drive, grit and aggression at high drive. **How to use it:** a saturator on a bass or 808 makes it heard on small speakers; a little on drums adds punch; use Mix to blend in the clean sound (parallel distortion).

In this chapter: **Overdrive**, **Distortion**, **Distortion II**, **Phase Distortion**, **Fuzz**, **Bitcrusher**, **Clip Distortion**, **Saturator**.

## Overdrive

![Overdrive](img/fx-overdrive.jpg)

A pedal-style overdrive: mids pushed into a soft, slightly uneven clip, then a tone control.

### Controls

**Drive** (0 to 60 dB) · **Tone** (500 to 12000 Hz) · **Output** (-30 to 12 dB) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Level Compensation** (Off, On)

## Distortion

![Distortion](img/fx-distortion.jpg)

Harder distortion: lots of gain into a sharper clip.

### Controls

**Drive** (0 to 60 dB) · **Tone** (500 to 12000 Hz) · **Output** (-30 to 12 dB) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Level Compensation** (Off, On)

## Distortion II

![Distortion II](img/fx-distortion2.jpg)

Distortion II: pre gain, drive and tone (a high shelf before and after) through one of five circuits - Growl (a two-stage tube amp), Bity (an overdriven blues amp), Nasty (hard), Class AB soft and hard (transistor crossover distortion) - then output, mix and level compensation.

### Controls

**PreGain** (-24 to 24 dB) · **Drive** (0 to 100 %) · **Tone** (0 to 100 %) · **Type** (Growl, Bity, Nasty, Class AB Soft, Class AB Hard) · **Output Level** (-30 to 12 dB) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Level Compensation** (Off, On)

## Phase Distortion

![Phase Distortion](img/fx-phaseDistortion.jpg)

Phase Distortion: the input's lows (a resonant low pass at Cutoff) bend a short delay on the signal itself, so its own bass moves its phase; Intensity, the delay's reach, Phase Reverse (the right channel moves the other way), Monitor (hear just the filter), mix.

### Controls

**Monitor** (Off, On) · **Cutoff** (20 to 2000 Hz) · **Resonance** (0 to 100 %) · **Intensity** (0 to 100 %) · **Phase Reverse** (Off, On) · **Delay** (0.1 to 10 ms) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %)

## Fuzz

![Fuzz](img/fx-fuzz.jpg)

Fuzz: a biased, nearly square clip with a gate for the hiss between notes.

### Controls

**Fuzz** (0 to 60 dB) · **Bias** (0 to 100 %) · **Tone** (500 to 12000 Hz) · **Gate** (-100 to -20 dB) · **Output** (-30 to 12 dB)

## Bitcrusher

![Bitcrusher](img/fx-bitcrusher.jpg)

Fewer bits and a lower sample rate (aliasing on purpose), with a drive and fold/clip/wrap at the top.

> **How to use it:** Lo-fi and chiptune: fewer bits for grit, a lower rate for aliasing.

### Controls

**Drive** (0 to 24 dB) · **Resolution** (1 to 24 bit) · **Downsampling** (1 to 50 x) · **Mode** (Fold, Clip, Wrap) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Output** (-24 to 12 dB) · **Clip Level** (-24 to 0 dB)

## Clip Distortion

![Clip Distortion](img/fx-clipDistortion.jpg)

Clip Distortion: input gain, drive into an asymmetric clip (Symmetry) through its clip filter, then a high shelf (frequency and gain) and a low pass, mix and output.

### Controls

**Drive** (0 to 48 dB) · **Symmetry** (-100 to 100 %) · **Clip Filter** (200 to 20000 Hz) · **LP Filter** (500 to 20000 Hz) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Output Gain** (-30 to 12 dB) · **Input Gain** (-24 to 24 dB) · **High Shelving** (500 to 16000 Hz) · **Gain** (-24 to 24 dB)

## Saturator

![Saturator](img/fx-saturator.jpg)

Saturator: five models - Retro Tube, Modern Tube, Magnetic (tape), Squeeze (pushed compression) and Analog Preamp - each with two styles (clean or colourful; soft or hard press), drive, Bypass Below (the lows left untouched under a frequency), level in and out, mix, and low and high cut filters (slope, resonance, before or after the saturation).

> **How to use it:** On a bass or 808: Magnetic or Retro Tube, Bypass Below at 80 Hz so the sub stays clean, drive until it's heard on a phone speaker.

### Controls

**Model** (Retro Tube, Modern Tube, Magnetic, Squeeze, Analog Preamp) · **Drive** (0 to 100 %) · **Style** (Clean, Colorful) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Level Out** (-24 to 12 dB) · **Level In** (-24 to 24 dB) · **Bypass Below** (Off, On) · **Bypass Below Freq** (20 to 500 Hz) · **Low Cut** (Off, On) · **Low Cut Slope** (6 dB/Oct, 12 dB/Oct, 24 dB/Oct) · **Low Cut Freq** (20 to 2000 Hz) · **Low Cut Resonance** (0 to 100 %) · **Low Cut Pre/Post** (Pre, Post) · **High Cut** (Off, On) · **High Cut Slope** (6 dB/Oct, 12 dB/Oct, 24 dB/Oct) · **High Cut Freq** (1000 to 20000 Hz) · **High Cut Resonance** (0 to 100 %) · **High Cut Pre/Post** (Pre, Post)
