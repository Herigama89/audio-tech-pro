# Utility Effects

Tools for fixing and testing: level, polarity, mono, test tones.

In this chapter: **Gain**, **Test Oscillator**.

## Gain

![Gain](img/fx-gain.jpg)

Gain: level up or down, invert the phase of either side, swap left and right, sum to mono (or keep just the left or right), and balance. The utility for fixing polarity and checking mono.

> **How to use it:** Mono on the master checks how the mix sounds on a phone speaker; Phase invert fixes two mics cancelling each other.

### Controls

**Gain** (-96 to 24 dB) · **Phase L** (Normal, Inverted) · **Phase R** (Normal, Inverted) · **Swap L/R** (Off, On) · **Mono** (Off, Mono, Left Only, Right Only) · **Balance** (-100 to 100)

## Test Oscillator

![Test Oscillator](img/fx-testOsc.jpg)

A test tone, noise or a sweep, in place of (or added to) the track.

### Controls

**Waveform** (Sine, Square, Saw, Triangle, White Noise, Pink Noise, Sweep) · **Frequency** (20 to 20000 Hz) · **Level** (-60 to 0 dB) · **Sweep Time** (0.5 to 30 s) · **Signal** (Replace, Add)
