# EQ Effects

EQ (equalization) turns frequency ranges up or down: less mud, more air, a clearer vocal. **How to use it:** cut narrow bands to remove problems, boost wide bands gently to add character. Cut lows below what an instrument needs with a low cut (80-100 Hz on vocals and guitars). Use the analyzer to see where the energy is, but decide with your ears.

In this chapter: **Channel EQ**, **Single Band EQ**, **Console EQ**, **Graphic EQ**, **Tube EQ**, **Linear Phase EQ**, **Match EQ**.

## Channel EQ

![Channel EQ](img/fx-channelEq.jpg)

Channel EQ: the eight bands on both channels or just one side (Left, Right, Mid or Side); HQ runs them at twice the rate (no cramping near the top); the analyzer reads before or after.

## Console EQ

![Console EQ](img/fx-consoleEq.jpg)

A British-console style EQ on the channel's own panel: a stepped high pass (the blue knob), a low shelf and a mid bell (each a grey gain knob inside a silver frequency ring), a 12 kHz high shelf (the grey knob on its own), the red preamp gain (drive: the circuit's colour) and Level. The Ø button turns the polarity over, EQ takes the EQ in and out (its lamp shows it in), and the meter shows the output in dBu. Silk, as on the transformer preamps: RED adds harmonics to the highs and upper mids (presence, air), BLUE thickens the lows; press the lit one again for none. The harder the red gain drives it, the more Silk there is.

### Controls

**High Pass** (Off, 50 Hz, 80 Hz, 160 Hz, 300 Hz) · **Low Freq** (35 Hz, 60 Hz, 110 Hz, 220 Hz) · **Low** (-16 to 16 dB) · **Mid Freq** (360 Hz, 700 Hz, 1.6 kHz, 3.2 kHz, 4.8 kHz, 7.2 kHz) · **Mid** (-18 to 18 dB) · **High (12k)** (-16 to 16 dB) · **Drive** (0 to 10) · **Output** (-18 to 18 dB) · **EQ** (Out, In) · **Phase** (Normal, Invert) · **Silk** (Off, Red, Blue)

## Graphic EQ

![Graphic EQ](img/fx-graphicEq.jpg)

A ten-band graphic EQ (31 Hz to 16 kHz, 12 dB of boost or cut each): slide the caps along their slots, to the right to boost, to the left to cut; the 1 kHz cap is red. IN takes the EQ in and out (its lamp lights while in).

## Tube EQ

![Tube EQ](img/fx-tubeEq.jpg)

A passive tube program EQ on its blue panel: low boost and low attenuation at the same frequency (CPS: 20, 30, 60 or 100 Hz; boost both for the famous tight low end), a high boost (KCS: 3 to 16 kHz) with its bandwidth (sharp to broad), a separate high attenuation (ATTEN SEL: 5, 10 or 20 kHz). The IN switch takes the EQ in and out; OFF/ON switches the tubes (the lamp lights): on, the tube stage adds its warmth; off, it's clean.

### Controls

**Low Freq** (20 Hz, 30 Hz, 60 Hz, 100 Hz) · **Low Boost** (0 to 10) · **Low Atten** (0 to 10) · **High Freq** (3 kHz, 4 kHz, 5 kHz, 8 kHz, 10 kHz, 12 kHz, 16 kHz) · **High Boost** (0 to 10) · **Bandwidth** (0 to 10) · **Atten Sel** (5 kHz, 10 kHz, 20 kHz) · **High Atten** (0 to 10) · **Output** (-12 to 12 dB) · **In** (Out, In) · **Tubes** (Off, On)

## Linear Phase EQ

![Linear Phase EQ](img/fx-linearEq.jpg)

Linear Phase EQ: the same eight bands as the Channel EQ (low and high cut with slopes up to 48 dB/oct, two shelves, four bells), but as a linear-phase filter, so nothing is shifted in time - the choice for mastering and for parallel or multi-mic sources. It adds a little latency.

> **How to use it:** Use it on the master or on drum buses, where phase shifts between tracks would smear the sound.

## Match EQ

![Match EQ](img/fx-matchEq.jpg)

Match EQ: learns the average spectrum of a reference (a song you like - the Template) and of your track (Current), then sets a filter that makes yours sound like it. Apply sets how much (negative values do the opposite), Smoothing how fine the curve is, Phase linear or minimum; four bands of your own on top.

> **How to use it:** Load a reference song as the Template, play your mix to learn Current, then set Apply to 30-60% for a gentle match.

### Controls

**Apply** (-100 to 200 %) · **Smoothing** (0 to 100 %) · **Phase** (Linear, Minimum) · **Analyzer** (Off, On) · **Clear Template** (Off, On) · **Clear Current** (Off, On) · **Gain** (-24 to 24 dB) · **Template File** (0 to 100000)

Each band (4, 1 to 4): Freq, Gain, Q, On/Off

The template curve (31, 20 to 20.2k): Level

The current curve (31, 20 to 20.2k): Level
