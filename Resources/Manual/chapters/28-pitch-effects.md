# Pitch Effects

Pitch effects tune, shift and transform voices and instruments. **How to use them:** Pitch Correction on a lead vocal (set the song's key and a response fast for the modern hard-tuned sound, slow for natural); Vocal Transformer for deep or high character voices; Pitch Shifter for octaves and harmonies.

In this chapter: **Pitch Correction**, **Vocal Transformer**, **Pitch Shifter**.

## Pitch Correction

![Pitch Correction](img/fx-pitchCorrection.jpg)

Pitch correction: each note pulled to the nearest note of the key, as fast as the Response says.

> **How to use it:** Set the key and scale of the song. A fast response gives the hard-tuned effect of modern trap and reggaeton; a slower one sounds natural.

### Controls

**Key** (C, C#, D, D#, E, F, F#, G, G#, A, ...) · **Scale/Chord** (Chromatic, Major, Minor, Major Pentatonic, Minor Pentatonic, Harmonic Minor, Melodic Minor, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, ...) · **Response** (1 to 400 ms) · **Detune** (-50 to 50 cents) · **Amount** (0 to 100 %) · **Bypass All** (Off, On) · **Pitch Range** (Normal, Low) · **Tolerance** (0 to 100 cents) · **Reference** (-50 to 50 cents) · **Keyboard** (Edit Scale, Bypass Notes) · **C in Scale** (Off, On) · **C# in Scale** (Off, On) · **D in Scale** (Off, On) · **D# in Scale** (Off, On) · **E in Scale** (Off, On) · **F in Scale** (Off, On) · **F# in Scale** (Off, On) · **G in Scale** (Off, On) · **G# in Scale** (Off, On) · **A in Scale** (Off, On) · **A# in Scale** (Off, On) · **B in Scale** (Off, On) · **Bypass C** (Off, On) · **Bypass C#** (Off, On) · **Bypass D** (Off, On) · **Bypass D#** (Off, On) · **Bypass E** (Off, On) · **Bypass F** (Off, On) · **Bypass F#** (Off, On) · **Bypass G** (Off, On) · **Bypass G#** (Off, On) · **Bypass A** (Off, On) · **Bypass A#** (Off, On) · **Bypass B** (Off, On) · **Mode** (Studio, Live)

## Vocal Transformer

![Vocal Transformer](img/fx-vocalTransformer.jpg)

Pitch and formants moved on their own (a deeper voice that isn't slowed, a higher one that isn't a chipmunk), or a robot.

> **How to use it:** Pitch down with formants kept for a deep voice; robot mode for effects.

### Controls

**Pitch** (-24 to 24 st) · **Formant** (-12 to 12 st) · **Mode** (Natural, Robot) · **Pitch Base** (C1, C#1, D1, D#1, E1, F1, F#1, G1, G#1, A1, ...) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Tracking** (-100 to 100 %) · **Glide** (0 to 500 ms) · **Grain Size** (Auto, Small, Normal, Large) · **Formants** (Process Always, Keep Unvoiced) · **Detune** (-50 to 50 cents)

## Pitch Shifter

![Pitch Shifter](img/fx-pitchShifter.jpg)

Shifts anything (chords, drums) by two crossfading delay taps sweeping through a window.

### Controls

**Semitones** (-24 to 24 st) · **Cents** (-100 to 100) · **Window** (20 to 150 ms) · **Mix** (0 to 100 %) · **Delay** (0 to 100 ms) · **Timing** (Manual, Drums, Speech, Vocals, Pitch Tracking) · **Crossfade** (5 to 100 %) · **Stereo Link** (Normal, Invert) · **Latency Comp** (Off, On)
