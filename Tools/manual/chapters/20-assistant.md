# The Claude Assistant

Click **Claude** in the control bar (**View > Claude Assistant**) for a panel where you can ask questions about your project - or tell it what to do, in your own words.

![The Claude assistant](img/assistant.jpg)

## Setting it up

The assistant uses your own **Anthropic API key**, and its use is billed to your Anthropic account. Click **Settings** in the panel, paste your key (it starts with `sk-ant-`) and choose a model: **Sonnet** is fast and capable (recommended), **Opus** is the most capable but slower and costs more, **Haiku** is the quickest and cheapest.

## What it can do

It can read your whole project (tempo, tracks, regions, mixer, effects) and change it:

- Set the tempo, the cycle, the click and count-in; play, stop, go to a bar.
- Add, change and delete tracks: names, faders, pans, mute, solo, record arm.
- Write MIDI, read the notes of a part, or generate drums, bass, chords and a melody in a genre.
- Set the channel strip EQ and dynamics, add and set effects, add sends to buses, set the master bus compressor and the master volume.
- Run any menu command.
- **Analyze the mix** - it renders the song and measures every track (peak, loudness and frequency balance), then tells you what to fix, or fixes it.

## Examples

- "Make a trap beat at 140 BPM in F minor."
- "Add a reverb bus and send the vocals and the keys to it."
- "Why does my mix sound muddy?"
- "Turn the hi-hats down 3 dB and pan them a little left."
- "How do I record several takes and pick the best parts?"

Each change it makes is one step you can undo with `Cmd/Ctrl+Z`. **New chat** starts a new conversation.
