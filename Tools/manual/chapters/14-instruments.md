# Instruments

An **instrument track** plays its MIDI through an instrument. To choose it, click the **instrument key** on the track header (it shows the instrument's name) or the **input slot** at the top of its mixer strip. The menu offers:

- **Show synth controls / Show plugin window** - opens the current instrument.
- **Tech Synth** - the simple built-in synth.
- **Nemix (wavetable synth)** - the big synth with hundreds of sounds and the recorded instruments. See the next chapter.
- **Drum Kit** - a built-in drum kit.
- **Sampler...** - plays any audio file of yours across the keyboard.
- **Plugins** - your own instrument plugins (VST3, AU, CLAP), by maker.

## Tech Synth

A classic one-oscillator synth: **Wave** (Saw, Square, Triangle, Sine), a low-pass filter (**Cutoff**, **Resonance**), an envelope (**Attack**, **Decay**, **Sustain**, **Release**) and **Volume**. Quick basses, leads and pads.

## Drum Kit

A drum kit on the General MIDI drum notes (kick on note 36, snare on 38, closed hi-hat on 42 and so on), so any standard drum pattern plays on it. **Tune** (up or down 12 semitones), **Decay** (shorter or longer hits) and **Volume**. The Drummer and the MIDI generator write for these notes too.

## Sampler

Choose an audio file (a note, a hit, a vocal chop) with **Choose Sound...**. It plays across the keyboard, pitched from the **Root Key** (the note that plays it as recorded), with **Attack**, **Release** and **Volume**.

## The Library - patches

Press `Y` (or the **Library** key) for the Library: **patches** - a track's whole sound (instrument, channel strip, inserts and sends) saved to put on other tracks. Select a track, click a patch, and it's loaded. Save your own from the strip's **Setting** slot.

## The Loop Browser

Press `O` for the Loop Browser. Choose a folder of loops and samples, click one to hear it, and drag it onto a track. Loops at another tempo can be fitted to the song's tempo.

## Smart Controls

`B` shows eight knobs for the selected track's instrument and channel strip. Right-click a knob to give it any other parameter.
