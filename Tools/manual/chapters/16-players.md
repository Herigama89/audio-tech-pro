# Session Players and Generate

## Session players

**Track > New Drummer Track**, **New Bass Player Track** and **New Keyboard Player Track** add a musician who plays along with your song, in its tempo and key. Select the player's region to show its editor under the tracks:

![The Drummer editor](img/drummer.jpg)

- **Genre** - Hip-Hop, Trap, Drill, Pop, R&B, Lo-fi, House, Techno, Afrobeats, Reggaeton or Rock.
- **The square** - drag the dot: left is **Simple**, right is **Complex**; up is **Loud**, down is **Soft**.
- **Fills** - how often it plays a fill. **Swing** - how much it swings.
- **Drummer parts** - switch **Kick & Snare**, **Hi-Hats**, **Cymbals**, **Toms** and **Percussion** on or off (play just the hats, say).
- **New Take** - the same settings, played a new way.

Make a region longer or shorter, or copy it to other parts of the song, and the player fills it. Different regions can have different settings: quieter in the verse, busier in the chorus. The bass and keyboard players follow the song's key.

## Generate MIDI

**Track > Generate MIDI...**, or the **Generate** key, writes a whole idea - drums, bass, chords and a melody - in a genre's style.

![The MIDI generator](img/generator.jpg)

- **Genre** - Hip-Hop (boom bap: swung drums, dusty seventh chords, a walking bass), Trap, Drill, Pop, R&B, Lo-fi, House, Techno, Afrobeats, Reggaeton and Rock.
- **Key** - root and Major or Minor (it starts in the song's key if it has one).
- **Length** - how many bars.
- **Parts** - Drums, Bass, Chords, Melody.
- **Variation** - low: a steady loop; high: more fills, rolls and changes.
- **Use the genre's tempo** - sets the project to the genre's usual tempo.
- **Replace what was generated before** - each part's new notes replace the last ones, on the same tracks.
- **Generate** - adds a new set of parts at the playhead's bar. **Try another** - replaces the parts you just made with a new take.

Drums use the standard General MIDI drum notes, so any drum instrument plays them. Then choose your own sounds - in Nemix, for example - and edit the notes in the piano roll.

## The Claude assistant can help too

Ask Claude "add a reggaeton beat and a bass line in A minor" and it writes and places the parts. See *The Claude Assistant*.
