# Screen Recording

**View > Record Screen...** (or the **Screen** key) records the app - or the whole screen - to a video with the mix's sound. Use it for tutorials, beat videos and social media.

![Screen recording](img/screen-record.jpg)

- **Record** - Audio Tech Pro's windows, or the whole screen.
- **Size** - up to 4K (2160p). **Frame rate** - 30 or 60 fps.
- **Audio** - the DAW's output, or the DAW's output plus a mic (your voice explaining).
- **Mic input** and **Mic level** - which interface input is the mic, and how loud.
- **Save to** - the folder for the videos.

Click **Start Recording**. The sound comes straight from the mix, so it's clean; the mic is only in the video, not in your speakers. Click the **Screen** key again to stop. The video is an MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio).

The first time on a Mac, macOS asks you to allow screen recording (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording). Allow it, then start again.
